## Academic Profile

I am a doctoral researcher in Marine Biology at the University of Haifa, studying the cellular and molecular mechanisms that control skeletogenesis in sea urchin embryos. My research examines how vesicle trafficking, cytoskeletal remodeling, and actomyosin regulation work together to support the formation and organization of the embryonic skeleton.

My work combines live-cell imaging, fluorescence and confocal microscopy, quantitative image analysis, and experimental perturbations. Through these approaches, I aim to understand how developing cells coordinate intracellular transport, cell behavior, and mineral deposition during embryogenesis.
